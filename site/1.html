Guidelines

Ticket Verification

Carry your e-ticket (QR code) on your phone or a printout. Tickets are scanned at the gate and are valid for one entry only.

ID Proof Requirement

A valid government photo ID matching the name on the booking is mandatory for entry.

Prohibited Items

Outside food and drinks, sharp objects, lighters, banners with offensive content, and large bags are not allowed inside the stadium.
